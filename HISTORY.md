# History

## 2026-10-18

Added a Goals tab for per-ISO-week task lists with click-or-drag progress bars, week navigation, and a copy of last week's unfinished items. Goals are stored in settings.json and in a Goals section of the Obsidian markdown file, and weeks whose Monday falls before the current financial year are archived and cleared with the financial-year reset.

Added two independent tick marks on each calendar day, drawn as a green lobe and a red lobe beside the date. Each mark can be named from a legend under the weekly goal. Marks are stored with that day's hours in the local JSON file and in the Obsidian markdown table, and they are archived or cleared with the financial-year reset.
